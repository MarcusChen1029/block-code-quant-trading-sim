# Zones (step 2 of 2): swing structure, order blocks, fair value gaps — design note (2026-10-07)

Native TypeScript zone indicators (src/indicators/zones.ts), offered as blocks in a **Zones** toolbox category
(src/blocks/zoneIndicators.ts) and drawn on the chart as boxes / levels (src/ui/zonePrimitive.ts). Built on the
step-1 signal model: each indicator has a numeric `ind_` block (output dropdown) and a Boolean `sig_` block (signal
dropdown) with shared parameters.

**Order-block definitions vary between traders** (ICT, LuxAlgo, "smart money" courses and others disagree on
which candle, wick vs body, what invalidates a block and whether a liquidity sweep is required). This app implements
ONE fixed, documented rule set, listed exactly below, with knobs for the common variations. It will not match every
TradingView script.

## No look-ahead, no repainting (hard rule)
- Every value at bar t depends only on candles 0..t; nothing already reported is ever revised. Tests compute each
  indicator on bars[0..t] and on the full series and require identical outputs, signals and zone states at every t
  (520-bar fixtures: trend, chop, gaps, flat, integer ties; src/indicators/zones.test.ts).
- A swing pivot at bar i needs `right` later bars, so it is KNOWN only on the close of bar i + right; outputs and
  signals use it only from then on. The drawn level / box starts at its origin candle (drawing only).

## Definitions
**Swing structure** (`swing`; params left 5, right 5)
- Pivot high at bar i: high[i] > high of each of the `left` bars before it AND high[i] >= high of each of the
  `right` bars after it (a flat top marks its FIRST bar). Needs `left` bars of history. Pivot low mirrored.
- Outputs: last swing high / last swing low (price of the latest confirmed pivot, na until one is known).
- Break of structure up: a bar CLOSES strictly above the most recent confirmed swing high that has not been
  broken yet; fires once, then that level is spent. A newer confirmed swing high replaces an unbroken older one
  (the old level ends as "superseded"). Down mirrored. Signals also: swing high / low confirmed (on the confirming bar).
- Drawn: dashed level from the pivot candle until broken / superseded (SH red, SL green); BOS markers (arrows).

**Order block** (`orderblock`; params swing length 5, lookback 10, body only 0, max age 300, max active 5)
- Bullish: on a bullish BOS bar (close above the last confirmed unbroken swing high, swing structure with
  left = right = swing length), the LAST bearish candle (close < open) among the `lookback` bars BEFORE the BOS bar.
  Zone = that candle's [low, high], or its body [min(open, close), max(open, close)] when "body only" = 1.
  Bearish: mirror (last bullish candle, close > open, before a bearish BOS). No such candle -> no block. A candle that
  is already the origin of an active block of the same side is not used twice.
- Known on the BOS bar's close (that bar fires "New bullish order block").
- Mitigated when a LATER bar closes beyond the far edge (bullish: close < bottom; bearish: close > top): removed,
  "... mitigated" fires. Also removed (no signal) when more than `max age` bars have closed since it became known,
  or when a side would hold more than `max active` (oldest first).
- Outputs: nearest active bullish block (top, bottom) = among bullish blocks not entirely above the close, the one
  with the smallest distance max(0, close - top) (ties: most recent); nearest bearish mirrored; na when none.
- "Price inside": the bar's range overlaps an active block (low <= top and high >= bottom), using the blocks active at
  the bar's close (after this bar's mitigations, including one formed on this bar).

**Fair value gap** (`fvg`; params min gap % 0.05, max age 300, max active 5)
- Candles a = t-2, b = t-1, c = t. Bullish: a.high < c.low, zone [a.high, c.low]. Bearish: a.low > c.high, zone
  [c.high, a.low]. Equal prices are not a gap. Height must be >= min gap % of c.close (0 = any positive gap), compared with a tolerance of 1e-9 x close
  so a gap exactly at the decimal minimum counts despite float rounding.
- Known on c's close; drawn from the middle candle b. Mitigated when a later bar closes through the whole gap
  (bullish: close < bottom; bearish: close > top). Same max age / max active / nearest / inside rules as order blocks.
  Overlapping gaps are separate zones.

## Choices where the brief was silent
- Lifecycle order per bar: retire existing zones (mitigation, then age) -> form new ones -> outputs. A zone is never
  mitigated on its own formation bar; for FVGs that is impossible anyway (c closes at or beyond the gap's near edge).
- Zone indicators draw boxes, not lines: their numeric outputs (nearest zone edges) are not plotted. Only BOS signals
  have chart markers; "new / inside / mitigated" signals are visible as the boxes themselves (markers would repeat
  every bar). They still work in blocks.
- FVG boxes start at the middle candle; order-block boxes at the order-block candle; active zones extend to the last
  bar, ended ones are drawn faded up to their end bar.
- Params are numbers (Blockly fields): "body only" is 0/1.

## Registry / blocks
- `IndicatorBlockDef.zones?: { style: 'orderBlock' | 'fvg' | 'swing' }`; instances implement `zones()` returning the
  zone lifecycle events of the last step: `{ id, kind, top, bottom, startTime, knownTime, endTime | null, endReason?,
  label }`. Consumers upsert by id. Additive: nothing else in the registry changed.
- Hoisting unchanged: zone blocks are stepped every bar even inside an `if` that is not taken (tested).
- Example: "Zone example" button next to "Reset to example" (src/blocks/zoneExample.ts): buy 100 % equity when the
  bar is inside a bullish order block AND a bullish FVG is active (its bottom > 0, i.e. not na) AND flat; close when
  a bullish order block is mitigated.

## Chart
- One lightweight-charts v5 series primitive on the candle series (`attachPrimitive`, `paneViews` -> renderer drawing
  in bitmap space with the pixel ratios; zOrder 'bottom' so candles stay on top). Geometry is recomputed on every
  draw from `logicalToCoordinate` (time -> logical index by binary search over the bar times) and
  `priceToCoordinate`, so zoom / scroll / resize / DPR are handled by the chart's own redraws.
- Colours: order blocks green / red, FVGs lime / orange, swing levels dashed green / red; labels are fixed strings
  ("OB+", "FVG-", "SH", ...) drawn on the canvas. Ended zones fade.
- Caps: at most 200 boxes drawn (the most recently formed VISIBLE ones; off-screen zones are skipped first); each
  overlay keeps at most 600 zones (oldest ENDED ones forgotten; active ones are bounded by max active).
- Shown (a) automatically for zone blocks used by the strategy (existing auto-overlay path, also live sessions), and
  (b) via the top-bar toggles "Order blocks", "FVG", "Swing structure" (default settings; remembered in
  localStorage `quant-sim.overlays.v1`, as are the other picker overlays now). The same zones from two overlays
  (strategy + toggle with equal settings) are drawn once (`zoneKey`).
- Live: closed bars step the overlays; new zone events only request a redraw of the primitive (no series recreated).

## Performance / memory
- Per bar: pivot window O(left + right), order-block lookback O(lookback), zone lists O(max active). 10,000 bars
  through all three indicators: well under the 500 ms the test allows (typically a few ms).
- Memory is bounded by the parameters, not the bar count.

## Not in scope
Pine drawing objects / arrays / UDTs, liquidity sweeps, equal highs / lows, multi-timeframe, other chart types.
